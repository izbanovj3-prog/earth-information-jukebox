## Data and credits (short paragraph)

The Earth Information Center records play visualizations by NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio and NASA's Global Modeling and Assimilation Office (SVS 5313, 3912, 5552, 5011 and 5533); their frames were downloaded from SVS and analysed ahead of time. The data records use NASA GIBS imagery, acknowledged as "imagery provided by services from NASA's Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS), part of NASA's Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS)". The orchestra is VS Chamber Orchestra 2 Community Edition by Versilian Studios, released as CC0. Coastlines are © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). The code is Apache-2.0; part of it comes from the second team prototype and keeps its copyright notice (see NOTICE). This is an unofficial prototype for NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, not a NASA product, uses no NASA logos, and its sound is an interpretation of the image, not a measurement.
